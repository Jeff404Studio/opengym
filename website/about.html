About Ferrum

Ferrum is a gym and body-weight tracker managed by Genefty.

Log workouts, follow a weekly plan, track body weight, and optionally use an AI Coach that designs or revises your plan — without medical claims and without locking your training history behind a subscription wall.

Your data stays on your instance. Export a backup anytime. Passkey sign-in keeps each profile separate when several people share the same deployment.

Principles

  • Your data is yours. Local-first backups, no lock-in — leaving must always be one export away.
  • No telemetry. If a feature needs analytics to justify itself, it doesn't ship.
  • Managed by Genefty. Product, hosting and support are run by genefty.com.
  • Coach is optional. Core workout tracking works even when AI is slow or offline.
